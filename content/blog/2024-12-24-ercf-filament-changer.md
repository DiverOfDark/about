I built this thing for the printer. In short: an eRCF v2 (enraged rabbit carrot feeder), an automated filament changer for the printer.

![Red and black ERCF filament changer sitting on top of the Voron printer](/img/blog/ercf-filament-changer-1.jpg)

The mechanics are assembled, I spent several days printing everything it needs, and here it all is together. The new electronics respond over the CAN bus, which makes the printer more and more like a modern car: several devices (this is already the third!) talking to each other over a CAN bus, and a head computer controlling the whole thing.

Still, I have a feeling that commissioning will take about a month, if not more. Compensating for all the unevenness of the printed parts will not be easy. And in places I can plainly see how crooked it is, unfortunately :(

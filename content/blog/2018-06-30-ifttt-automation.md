A surprising number of things can be done with the old and well-known service IFTTT.com (If This Then That).

I first heard about it something like ten years ago, but I started using it actively in the last year, to automate my life.

The use cases I have:

- All SMS from my phone are sent to my finance tracking service (BudgetTracker).
- Collecting data about my weight from the FitBit cloud and sending it to an analytics service that is convenient for me (ironically, that is BudgetTracker too).
- Creating recurring tasks in Trello.

If you have interesting cases of automating your life, tell me, I would be curious to know.

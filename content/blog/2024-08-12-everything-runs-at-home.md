Continuing the self-hosting story: I built a k3s cluster at home out of three mini PCs.

I think I will write a series about it, because there is plenty to tell. As a teaser I put a status page on the internet, so you can get a rough idea of the stack.

I moved every service I used from the rented server onto it and sent the cancellation. Everything is hosted at home now.

The nicest part: the backups work. I have already rebuilt the cluster from scratch once without losing a single byte.

A few small things are left to tidy up, but otherwise it works, and I am proud of myself. And of course all of the infrastructure is code, on GitHub :)
